Expand description

Outcome of ThreadedExecutableInstruction::execute_threaded().

Unlike ExecutionResult, this is produced exactly once, by the handler that stops the chain and is not on the per-instruction path.

The instruction fetcher travels through the chain by value and comes back here to remain available, exactly as it would be after ExecutableInstruction::execute().
